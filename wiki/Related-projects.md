# Related projects

## Weather Network Uploader

https://github.com/lancer73/ha-weather-uploader

Uploads data from your Home Assistant weather sensors to weather networks:
Weather Underground, WOW-BE, CWOP, PWSWeather, Windy and OpenWeather.

It pairs naturally with this integration — one pushes your own observations out,
the other pulls forecasts and air quality back in. Contributing station data to OpenWeather is
also how the maintainer got onto the Startup plan — upload for a while, then
ask at info@openweathermap.org. See [OpenWeather plans](OpenWeather-plans).
